## What This Does

Find companies that need what you sell, research them, and draft personalized outreach — all in parallel. This tool replaces hours of manual prospecting with a single command.

## How to Use

The user will tell you what kind of companies or people they're looking for. Run the workflows below.

---

## Workflow 1: Find Leads

When the user says "find me leads", "who needs [product]", or describes a target customer, run ALL of these in parallel:

**A. Companies Hiring (Indeed)**
Use search_jobs to find companies actively hiring for roles related to the user's product:
- Search the user's target keywords + "remote US" (or their specified location)
- Companies investing in hiring for a domain = they have budget and need

**B. Market Research (Web Search)**
Search for:
- "[user's target market] companies 2026"
- "[user's product category] buyers"
- "companies that need [what user sells]"
- "[user's vertical] market trends 2026"

**C. Existing Network (Gmail — if connected)**
Search for prior conversations with potential prospects:
- People who asked about the user's product/service
- Professional contacts in relevant industries

Compile results into a prioritized prospect list:

PROSPECT LIST
=============

HIGH PRIORITY (strong signals):
1. [Company] — [Why they need it] — [Signal: hiring/funding/news]
2. [Company] — [Why they need it] — [Signal]
3. [Company] — [Why they need it] — [Signal]

MEDIUM PRIORITY (likely fit):
4-7. [Companies with context]

EXPLORE (worth researching):
8-10. [Companies with potential]

## Workflow 2: Research a Prospect

When the user says "research [company]" or "tell me about [company]", run in parallel:

- Web search: "[company] products 2026"
- Web search: "[company] recent news funding"
- Web search: "[company] leadership team"
- Gmail search: any prior contact with this company (if connected)

Produce a prospect brief:

PROSPECT BRIEF: [Company Name]
================================

COMPANY: [what they do, size, industry]
RECENT NEWS: [funding, launches, hires]
DECISION MAKERS: [names/titles if found]
WHY THEY NEED [USER'S PRODUCT]: [specific pain points]
BEST OUTREACH ANGLE: [personalization hook]

---

## Workflow 3: Draft Outreach

When the user says "draft an email to [prospect]", create personalized outreach:

**Email structure:**
Subject: [Specific + relevant, under 50 chars]

[Name] —

[One sentence referencing something specific about them. Show homework.]

[One sentence about the problem they likely face.]

[One sentence about how you solve it — specific to their situation.]

[Clear ask — one CTA only.]

Best,
[User's name]

**Rules:**
- Under 100 words total
- No "I hope this finds you well"
- No generic value props — tie to THEIR specific situation
- Reference a deadline, news item, or hiring signal
- One ask, not three
- Create as Gmail DRAFT — never send directly
- ALWAYS get user approval before sending anything
